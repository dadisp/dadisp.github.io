Agm

A personal social media scheduler

Privacy Policy Terms of Service Bahasa Indonesia

Plan posts once, and let them go out on time

Agm lets its owner write posts, plan them on a calendar, and publish them on schedule to the owner's own Telegram channels, Facebook Pages, Instagram accounts and YouTube channels.

Agm runs on the owner's own computer. It is not a hosted service, and it has no public sign-up.

Why Agm asks for access to a Google account

Agm uses YouTube API Services, and only for the owner's own channels:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): to upload the videos the owner has scheduled.
  • View the YouTube account (youtube.readonly): to read the channel's ID and name, so Agm can show which channel is connected.

Google data is kept only on the owner's computer. It is not sold and is not used for advertising. The Privacy Policy has the full details.

Bahasa Indonesia

Agm membantu pemiliknya menulis post, menyusunnya di kalender, dan menerbitkannya sesuai jadwal ke channel Telegram, Page Facebook, akun Instagram, dan channel YouTube miliknya sendiri.

Agm berjalan di komputer pemiliknya. Agm bukan layanan online dan tidak menerima pendaftaran umum.

Kenapa Agm meminta akses ke akun Google

Agm memakai YouTube API Services, hanya untuk channel milik pemiliknya:

  • Mengunggah video (youtube.upload): untuk mengunggah video yang sudah dijadwalkan pemilik.
  • Melihat akun YouTube (youtube.readonly): untuk membaca ID dan nama channel, agar Agm bisa menunjukkan channel mana yang terhubung.

Data Google hanya disimpan di komputer pemilik, tidak dijual, dan tidak dipakai untuk iklan. Rinciannya ada di Kebijakan Privasi.
